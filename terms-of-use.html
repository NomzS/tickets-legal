Subscriptions and in-app purchases

  • Memory Tickets Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or annual, price shown at purchase) that unlocks all premium features and removes ads. It renews automatically at the end of each billing period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before renewal. Manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings — cancelling stops future charges but keeps Pro active until the end of the period you already paid for. Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Memory Tickets Pro Lifetime is a one-time, non-renewing purchase that unlocks the same premium features permanently on the account that bought it. Nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.
  • Remove Ads is a one-time, non-renewing purchase that removes ads without unlocking other Pro features.

All three are billed and processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store and are subject to their respective terms and refund policies. Use the “Restore Purchases” option in the app to restore a prior purchase or active subscription on a new or reinstalled app.
